# Public edition 2 — A Knight Needs a Sidekick

Promise: help an aspiring knight prepare, discover whether his confidence survives a real test, and leave with an honest account without crushing his hope.

Source: Cervantes, Don Quixote I.1, Ormsby. Rechecked against https://www.gutenberg.org/files/996/996-h/996-h.htm#ch1. Reading boundary ends before the first departure. The original public package remains at its original path; this edition uses revisions/2. Classroom publication and saved attempts are untouched.

Fixed sequence: reading and land sales → literary arguments → decision to become a knight → ancestral armour → pasteboard helmet → two sword cuts, first destroys it → iron reinforcement, no retest → Rocinante (four days) → Quixote (eight days; Amadis pattern) → Dulcinea (Aldonza). No chance determines source events. The visiting helper and all spoken dialogue are invented. Materials and helmet destruction are described; supported sword prop and arm strikes show his movement without inventing an animated breakable object.

## Three writing passes

Opening: three short book beats, one question, two transition beats, then an onstage argument. Place, period, roles and practical task precede the preparations. Retains the requested airport, marmalade, travel pillow and overpriced joke.

Cause and effect: admiration becomes a decision; decision requires equipment; testing destroys his work; disappointment motivates reinforcement; avoidance of another failure produces refusal to retest. Naming builds on this contrast between a pleasing idea and what has actually changed.

Trimming: replaced the invented rolling cart/rescue and luck branches with Chapter 1's actual helmet test. Reduced definition-heavy narration to the two terms assessed, after the player experiences them. Explicit passage of days prevents the naming from appearing instantaneous. Quiet disappointment after the test gives its cost room; playful naming restores energy before the account.

Acting: paired challenge and book handoff with existing polished barber; kneel and crouch while working; visible sword and two strikes; shocked silence; held folded arms at refusal; reaching horse inspection; cheer, bows and laughter. Wide shots retain hands and lower-body movement; source excerpt and knowledge check remain readable without motion. No new runtime features or artwork.
