# The Worst Detective in the Book Club

English Corner · due 2026-11-04 · original story · 10–15 minutes plus assessment.
Voice: warm, self-questioning adolescent. Kevin's wish to look impressive becomes actual curiosity; humour points at his confident explanations, not language learners.
Promise: Kevin wants to impress a book club, but their missing hamster needs careful observation rather than impressive vocabulary. Mei needs her pet safely home; Lin wants her favourite books taken seriously and a friend to discuss them with.
Care: Kevin quietly closes the door and blocks its gap before performing detective confidence. No chase, animal distress, or human villain. Search is a few minutes, not days; reading habit growth is a later epilogue.

Story circle: confident outsider → wants to impress → taxi to book club → overhard reading and search → readable mystery reveals how to look → careless suspicion hurts Mei → combine three clues and recover Crumb → borrow next volume and return repeatedly. Hamster becomes honorary academic supervisor.

Clue contract: before final deduction EVERY route knows (1) garden mud stops outside the closed door; (2) fresh triangular bite gaps in a yellow paper bookmark, matching scraps leading toward the low cupboard; (3) seed shells and faint rustling at its dark floor-level gap. Picture-supported repeated 'beneath' helps follow a book clue but never substitutes for evidence. Red hat is misleading because gardener left before carrier opened; the seven-hat gardener is in the series, not the real room. Final options: beneath cupboard / garden / high shelf. Wrong choices receive specific evidence and a chance to revise in dialogue; no narrative points.

Hepting brief questions, warm stillness.

Scripts use supported poses; hamster/carrier/book illustrations described in text and backdrop rather than claiming an unsupported animal rig.

Learning: easy interesting sustained reading, context/pictures, selective lookup, recurring vocabulary and manageable routine. No instant-fluency promise or moral score. Terms extensive reading and comprehensible input stay in optional teacher notes/quiz explanations.
