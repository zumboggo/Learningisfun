# The Stolen Camel · authoring plan

Primary voice: immediate drama. Competing claims and an approaching hearing drive the story. First-person student narrator replaces Kevin explicitly at David's request; no named, gendered or visible player avatar.

Student joins a Muzayna household as a welcomed guest, helps carry water, and accompanies the camel owner. This is temporary hospitality, not an invented formal tribal adoption. Salim (host/helper), the owner, and a spokesperson among the enslaved people have authored dialogue. The student helps communicate claims; Umar's outcome never depends on a modern saviour. Umar is present through narrated decisions, not an invented portrait or attributed speeches. No Prophet depiction.

Historical spine: Malik's Muwatta, Judgements 36.38 (Arabic numbering 1441), records people enslaved by Hatib taking/slaughtering a Muzayni man's camel, Umar initially ordering amputation, then identifying starvation and imposing 800 dirhams after the owner says he refused 400. Treat withdrawal of punishment as the traditional interpretation of the sequence, not an additional verbatim clause. Malik explicitly rejects doubling as his community's rule, preferring value at taking. No exact year, famine identification, courtroom transcript, invented loan, debt-enslavement origin, completed payment or emancipation is asserted. Stage courtyard/hearing in Medina region as dramatic setting; report does not fix exact venue.
