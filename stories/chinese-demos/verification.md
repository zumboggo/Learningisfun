# Teacher workshop Mandarin demos v1

Original episodes, available to every teacher through The Game → Examples for teachers and protected full-window routes. No class assignment or server-side student attempt is created. Packages/audio are public original teaching examples; progress is versioned and scoped to account + episode in local storage. Signing into another account cannot resume the first account's progress.

Validation: both packages have 32 complete routes, exactly five decisions per route, and ten distinct choice responses. All narration/dialogue/options have exact concatenating phrase annotations and cached Mandarin speech. Every named speaker is staged. Engine replay checks the stage after every step on all 64 routes. Each package plus its speech is below 1.8 MB; opening videos remain external to the package.

Playback: contrasting routes on 1280×900 desktop and 390×844 phone, both with normal motion and reduced motion, all eight completed. Tested independent support toggles, tap reveal, reload/resume (including a choice screen), and no quiz. Reviewed screenshots and corrected dark-mode text contrast; the classroom uses the existing higher-resolution manga scenery. Bowls, notes and furniture actions remain narrated; no claim of new handoff or sitting animation.

Language: reviewed sentence and chunk pinyin; corrected context-sensitive 教, 得, 肚, 还, 长, 只 and common neutral-tone words. The vocabulary benchmark uses David's current 1,063-word union (supplement v3), but these workshop originals are designed for mixed beginners with help, not as his next personalized 95–98% installment. No private word export is shipped.

Audio: 152 content-addressed Edge recordings, Mandarin only, exact voice/rate/pitch/volume/format in cache keys. Three bounded attempts for transient free speech-service failures; completed files reused. Generated teacher teaser recognized as 我也有點怕. Mystery generation included an extra filler, so its soundtrack was replaced with the exact approved Xiaoxiao recording; no paid retry. Both generated clips were 6 seconds at verified $0.01/second in cost/480p mode ($0.06 each), with a three-second first-frame hold added offline. Local ffmpeg checks verified nine seconds, decoded audio and the initial hold. Recognition is a check, not a guarantee of pronunciation quality.

Regression checks: 207 test files / 1,060 tests passed in the initial broad Vitest run; three existing exhaustive route tests and eight worker launches timed out during contention. All 11 affected files / 60 tests passed on a bounded two-worker rerun. All remaining npm-test backend suites passed. The new three component tests, relevant player/library tests, TypeScript, focused lint, package checks and production build passed. Browser failures caused by completion-screen race assumptions were corrected; the last timed-out phone route passed alone.
