# AP Language

Read the assigned text and teach rhetorical interpretation through a concrete problem. Preserve authentic quotations and optional source notes.

Mr. Hepting summons Didi through his secret WeChat mini-program. It passes through the classroom window and lands at the front, then drives through a spacetime rift over the CDIS soccer field. Give the arrival a place, year and immediate task, with verified period details where appropriate. The serious driver in sunglasses asks the four knowledge questions; three correct pays the fare home. Review/retry preserves story progress.

New episodes have no required closing analysis or choice-based learning score. Writing is optional/separate. For released Own English versions only, consult legacy/ap-language.md.
