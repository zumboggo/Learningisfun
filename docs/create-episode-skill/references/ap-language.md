# AP Language

Read the assigned text and teach rhetorical interpretation through a concrete problem. Preserve authentic quotations and optional source notes.

Mr. Hepting summons the generic magical taxi through his secret WeChat mini-program. Reuse the approved arrival and field-departure clips by default, following [the shared magical-taxi workflow](magical-didi.md). Add the `taxi_arrival` and `taxi_departure` labels at the corresponding readable beats; shared videos stay outside episode packages. Give the arrival a place, year and immediate task, with verified period details where appropriate. The serious driver in sunglasses asks the four knowledge questions; three correct pays the fare home. Review/retry preserves story progress.

New episodes have no required closing analysis or choice-based learning score. Writing is optional/separate. For released Own English versions only, consult legacy/ap-language.md.
