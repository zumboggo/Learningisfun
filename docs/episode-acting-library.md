## Held props

- `none`
- `phone`
- `sword`
- `bow`
- `lance`

Use `act Lin attack-blocked Jordan "Lin lunges; Jordan blocks."` for a coordinated action beat, or `act Lin bow "Lin bows."` for one character. Show both actors in distinct positions first. Paired actions face each other and default to a wide shot (or fitted `camera action Lead Target`); later dialogue uses the authored camera again. A caption is required (one line, no double quotes inside it). Actions pause until Continue; motion cannot change scores, variables or outcomes. Reload restores the caption and stage without replaying the action. Reduced motion skips movement; static images use whole-character motion. Polished/hybrid cartoon-v1 characters support visible props: `prop Lin phone`, `prop Lin sword`, `prop Lin bow`, `prop Lin lance`, `prop Lin none`. Equip only after show; scene, exit or a new show resets the prop. Prop actions auto-equip their object and leave it held. `phone-talk` and `phone-shout` also work as talking gestures while the phone stays at the ear. Ordinary give remains mimed. Use wide framing for weapons and projectiles; magic-beam clears the held object. Reduced motion shows static props and a static effect. Mounted-charge shows a horse and buckler for that caption only, with a levelled lance; use a solo wide shot. It does not simulate a hit or fall. Wince adds a pained flinch. Steady offers a supporting arm and preserves the existing resting poses; narrate any rise separately. Every action returns to its resting pose at its original marks. Use `rest Lin arms-crossed` for folded arms that stay folded during speech; use angry emotion for a stern expression. `act Lin cross-arms "Lin folds her arms."` is a finite gesture and frees both hands; follow with that rest to hold it. `lance-threat` levels the visible lance without a hit. Put props away before a held folded-arm pose. Use kneeling, crouched or sheltered rests to stay low; relaxed returns upright. Use a following line/exit to describe lasting injury or absence; a return to the mark is not a recovery in the story.

## Framing and atmosphere

See `docs/scene-continuity.md` for `perform`, `express` and `look`. Fit faces with `camera close Lin cut`, `camera listener Mei over 0.8`, or `camera speaker`; frame pairs with `camera two Lin Mei` and weapons with `camera action Lin Mei`. Cuts are instant; `over` accepts 0–3 seconds. Action framing cuts immediately to preserve contact geometry. Add `ambience hall|wind|rain|none`, `environment dust|rain|none`, and `sound swish|straw|thud|clang|step|chime`. Sound starts off with independent ambience/effects controls; voice ducks background sound. Scene changes reset ambience and environment. Reduced motion removes dust/rain and moving zooms. Sound cues are transient, never replayed on resume.

## Authoring

```story
story 2
label start
scene classroom
show Lin curious center
camera medium Lin
Lin [curious, question]: What changed your mind?
react Lin nod
Lin [relieved, reassure]: We can try again.
camera wide
I: I step back to see the whole room.
end
```

Emotion automatically selects a resting pose and talking gesture. Plain dialogue uses the current emotion. Optional `rest Lin attentive` overrides the resting pose. `react` plays once, then releases the pose. Speaking stops after a bounded interval and between dialogue; no speech synthesis is used. Breathing is a tiny chest expansion with grounded feet; blinks are spaced with varied timing. A talking gesture prepares, emphasizes, holds, then settles once; mouth shapes are small with pauses. Motion is disabled with reduced motion. Use close shots for a personal revelation, medium for conversation, and wide for arrivals or choices; `camera auto` restores automatic framing. Keep gestures sparse.

Whole-character moves: `enter`, `exit`, `left`, `center`, `right`, `turn`, `nod`, `shake`, `bounce`, `recoil`. Stage effects: `fade`, `closeup`, `shake`, `magic`. Prefer `react` for joint-based responses and `camera` for sustained framing. Flat image characters still work but cannot articulate.

## Current cartoon character contract

New characters default to polished SVG (`finish: "polished"`). The recurring cast uses `finish: "hybrid"` with an embedded WebP `head: {src, frame: [x,y,width,height]}` and animated SVG facial features. Head assets count toward the package budget and travel in ZIP exports. Missing finish preserves the legacy flat vector appearance. For example, a vector rig has `rig: {version: 2, preset: "cartoon-v1", style: "manga", palette: {skin: "#eac09c", hair: "#293338", shirt: "#b93f46"}}`. Palette values must be six-digit hex colours. The runtime draws the complete figure in one vector style, including curved arms and consistent hands and face. No bitmap body parts are required; recurring hybrid characters embed one illustrated head base. Optional appearance selects age (teen/adult), hair (layered/side-part/long/bob/cropped), glasses (none/round), outfit (uniform/teacher/tunic/robe), face (default/oval/round/angular), and facialHair (none/full). Optional palette glasses, beard and trim colours customize these details. Omitted appearance preserves Lin’s original design. See character-standard.md for constraints and the creation prompt. Keep the existing cue vocabulary.
