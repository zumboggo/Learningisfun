# The Question Journal · World Literature · v1

Two independently playable 12–15 minute opening encounters, assigned October 12, 2026. Both release at **2026-10-12T00:00:00Z (08:00 Asia/Shanghai)**, overriding Friday release only for these assignments. Teacher previews are available earlier. No Canvas assignments or grades are posted.

| Story | Text and concepts |
| --- | --- |
| A Knight Needs a Witness | Don Quixote I.1: books, armor, names; characterization, diction, parody |
| The Giants Have Sails | I.8: windmills through remounting; irony, juxtaposition, counterevidence, perspective |

The weathered journal comes from Mr. Hepting’s Peru airport gift shop. Three authored questions choose the opening; an optional personal question and final reflection are private writing, never live AI prompts. Each student travels alone, becomes the appointed chronicler, and meets Quixote. The second encounter also introduces Sancho without requiring the first episode. Cervantes visits the page margin three times in each story, with optional follow-ups. He does not inhabit the scene or establish an official interpretation.

Each story has two unscored commitments and four evidence checkpoints: Evidence, Interpretation, Language & Form, Connections. Each checkpoint offers two supported accounts worth 25 and an unsupported overclaim worth 5. Unencountered categories start at zero. Character reactions, curiosity, and successful persuasion earn no points. The journal asks exactly four MCQs; three correct allow return. Review/retry keeps choices and writing. Replays create separate attempts; prior completions remain intact.
