## Teaching review

- Common case: stage 1 explains the sheep's literal role and a supported symbolic reading before introducing allegory.
- Objective practice: stage 2 rehearsals require students to explain meaning with evidence; stage 3 requires an explanation connecting two events.
- Evidence beside explanation: sheep's repeated routines → comfortable dependence; leaving flock → risking security; rebuilding through work → response to setbacks.
- Missing move: stage 2 supplies the crystal claim and evidence, leaving only the explanatory link. Its second rehearsal removes the claim support as well.
- Fresh retries: the crystal-display risk question retries with selling the flock; the departure/theft/work allegory question retries with the merchant's fear/experiment/result sequence. All retries retain the same decision and supply scene context.
- Engagement: the opening asks why Santiago would risk a familiar livelihood; 'the sheep symbolize symbolism' exposes empty labeling, immediately repaired with evidence.
- Timing estimate, not student-tested: 4 minutes explanation/models/check reading; 4 minutes guided writing and model comparison; 4 minutes final drafting; 2 minutes feedback and revision; 1 minute transitions = 15 minutes. Ten minutes are protected for explanation, comparison, drafting, and revision. No countdown.
- Fresh final: two equivalent options, stones/guidance or postponed pilgrimage/action. Both supply all context and require a symbol plus two connected events/choices. No ending spoilers. No universal claims about religion.
- Outcomes and AI feedback use the existing backend without changes. No live AI or student work is needed for verification.
