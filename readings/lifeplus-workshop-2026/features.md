# Learning is Fun: Tools, Features, and What You Can Do With Them

A practical feature map for Lifeplus Workshop, November 2026. Start with a teaching problem, choose one useful tool, and add more when you need them. Features vary by account, class, assignment, and device. Special course activities are identified below rather than promised as universal tools.

## Get your account ready

- **One account for the workshop and your teaching:** participate as a learner inside Lifeplus Workshop and return to your own teaching workspace afterward.
- **Teacher access:** activate an existing account using David’s invitation at [Teacher access](https://zumboggo.github.io/Learningisfun/#/teacher-access); existing work stays with the account.
- **Workshop enrollment:** after signing in, open [Join workshop](https://zumboggo.github.io/Learningisfun/#/workshop) and enter the workshop class code David supplies. If already enrolled, this opens the class.
- **Two different codes:** the teacher invitation enables teaching tools; the workshop code enrolls you in David’s workshop. Neither is your login password.
- **Already a teacher?** Skip activation and join the workshop with your usual account.

## Classes and classroom management

- **Classes and join codes:** create a home for each class or section and invite students using their own accounts.
- **Class materials:** bring readings, decks, quizzes, presentations, and assigned activities together.
- **Roster and progress reports:** see membership and participation; use available activity reports to identify who needs support.
- **Class timer and Today’s Notes:** display time and organize the lesson’s instructions and resources.
- **Parent and substitute access:** use the class’s supported access options for their designated purposes; a parent account is not a teacher account.
- **Class banners and resource links:** make classes recognizable and put useful destinations close to the work.

## Reading, Copywork, and discussion

- **Texts:** paste a reading, import a supported file, or link to an external resource; assign it to the intended class.
- **Assigned Reading and Copywork labels:** identify how a text will be used and help students locate the right materials.
- **Reader controls:** adjust text size and use available supported versions to make demanding material more approachable.
- **Original files and images:** retain access to uploaded source documents and illustrations where provided.
- **Annotations and text discussion:** keep thinking close to the passage, compare interpretations, and connect claims to evidence.
- **Private question drafting:** give participants space to formulate questions before sharing them.
- **Published questions and voting:** surface questions the class wants to investigate; the teacher controls the available discussion stages.
- **Replies and reply rounds:** ask participants to respond to selected questions and to one another’s reasoning.
- **Text presentation:** bring shared passages and contributions into whole-class attention.
- **Copywork notebook:** students can collect copied language and observations; the workshop lesson also includes handwritten practice.
- **QFT prompts and discussions:** support question-driven inquiry while facilitating the question-type work and reflection explicitly.

## Practice and feedback

- **Flashcards:** create or import decks with focused prompts, answers, examples, hints, and tags.
- **Spaced review:** attempt recall before revealing the answer, then rate recall to schedule further practice.
- **Card presentation and analytics:** review together and inspect patterns in practice and recall.
- **Quizzes:** build or generate a draft, check answers and explanations, then publish and assign a focused check of understanding.
- **Interactive Lessons:** teach through short explanations, models, practice checks, hints, and fresh retry examples.
- **Lesson application and revision:** participants produce an answer and can receive rubric-based feedback through the lesson workflow; teachers can review progress and outcomes.
- **Writing prompts:** collect short explanations, predictions, arguments, or exit responses for teacher feedback and discussion.
- **Dangerous Writing:** a timed fluency challenge that ends after a five-second pause; keep important final drafts elsewhere and save work you want to retain.
- **Student Error Log:** record recurring writing problems and useful corrections for deliberate practice.
- **Peer-review activities:** structure feedback on classmates’ work, including presentation review where assigned.

## Planning and creating materials

- **Create studio:** draft supported teaching resources, inspect the result, revise it, and choose where to publish or assign it.
- **Planner / brainstorming board:** arrange ideas on movable cards for a lesson, unit, essay, or project; use backup and export tools to keep your work.
- **Weekly planner:** organize classes, timetables, teaching days, and lesson activities in calendar views.
- **Reusable routines and resource bank:** reuse an activity while adapting its lesson-specific copy.
- **Lesson timing and print views:** check the planned duration and keep a readable teaching plan.
- **Publish selected materials:** decide which resources students receive; private planning notes do not become assignments merely by appearing in a plan.
- **Annual planning import:** prepare and review structured plans from an external AI tool before applying them.

## Presentations, stories, and exploration

- **Presentation files and links:** keep slides and other resources with the class; check permissions on external destinations.
- **Live presentations and responses:** use supported presentation activities to gather answers during teaching.
- **The Game / story studio:** write or generate an editable episode, choose characters and settings, and preview it before publication.
- **Branching choices and quizzes:** let learners use knowledge in a story decision and check understanding afterward.
- **Character library and animation controls:** reuse a cast and adjust the presentation of scenes.
- **Story templates:** explore available frames such as neutral adventures, question journals, and teaching-stone activities.
- **Special course experiences:** Principles Portfolio, rhetorical-situation practice, and some story activities are configured for particular classes; they are examples of possibilities, not automatically assigned to every new class.

## Settings and practical limits

- **AI allowance and supported connections:** inspect your current allowance and available settings before generating; manual authoring remains an option.
- **Review AI drafts:** check facts, sources, suitability, answer keys, and instructions before publication.
- **Presentation-folder shortcut:** save your own folder link for quick access.
- **Sharing controls:** keep class materials private unless you intentionally enable public reading; external files have their own permissions.
- **Sync and exports:** wait for important work to sync and keep exports; offline access depends on what has already been loaded.

## Three useful paths for your first evening at home

**Start a class:** open [Classes](https://zumboggo.github.io/Learningisfun/#/classes), create your class, open its management controls, and find the student join code. Success means the correct class and section appear in your workspace.

**Assign a reading:** open [Texts](https://zumboggo.github.io/Learningisfun/#/texts), select **Add a Text**, add the source, choose the class and purpose, then save/publish as the form directs. Check its availability date and open it from the class. If missing, check publication, assignment, release date, and sync.

**Start a discussion:** open [Discussions](https://zumboggo.github.io/Learningisfun/#/discussions), select **Start discussion**, then **Text discussion**, the class, and its assigned reading. Introduce one stage at a time. Success means learners can find the same text and perform the contribution you have opened.

For the broader walkthrough, return to [A Guide to Learning is Fun App](https://zumboggo.github.io/Learningisfun/#/texts/wg_e34f03900e9494d9168dc5b13fd567dd?classId=5a726282-4771-4774-8213-d9329c124bbc). Begin with one real lesson, not every feature on this page.
