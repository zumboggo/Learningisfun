## Agreed scope

The readings are short workshop discussion handouts, not full illustrated historical readings. Existing app guide retained. No Canvas edits or participant role changes.

## Lesson design review

- Observable action: copy accurately, explain a specific writerly choice, transfer a move into original writing.
- Common case: a concrete verb giving a clock a consuming action; explain the effect before trying subtler contrast.
- Objective practice: stage 2's ungraded explanation rehearsal and stage 3's original library-closing sentence.
- Evidence beside explanation: “swallowed our last minute” paired with time as a hungry opponent.
- Missing move: explain the effect of a stubborn question before seeing the sample.
- Fresh retries: a groaning gate, rain on a roof, silence as a barrier, crowded room/absent person, unsigned form as a barrier, repeated speech openings.
- Final: choose a new institutional scene or garden scene; explain a detail and transfer one move.
- Timing: 2 minutes copy, 1 minute notice, 2 minutes model/explanation, 2 minutes explain and compare, 2 minutes transfer rehearsal/checks, 4 minutes fresh application and revision, 2 minutes transitions. About 9–11 minutes are participant action/comparison/revision. Allow extra time for accessibility or discussion; this is not a countdown.
- Assessment limit: no handwriting upload is available in this lesson; participants compare their copy and a partner/teacher checks it. AI assesses the written explanation and application, not unseen handwriting.
- The full Canvas rubric is a separate reference; its six-paragraph requirements are not imposed on this 15-minute lesson.
